The Bullpen

How to Play

  • Batting: drag anywhere to move the gold circle. Tap Contact or Power when the ball gets near the plate. If the ball is inside the circle and your timing is right, you hit it.
  • Above the circle's center hits flies. Below it hits grounders. Swing early to pull it, late to go the other way.
  • Pitches with no swing outside the white box are balls.
  • Pitching: pick a pitch, drag to aim, tap Pitch, then tap Release as the needle crosses the green mark. Tired pitchers miss more, so change them in the pause menu.
  • Your fielders and runners play by themselves.
  • Career: win games for gate money, train players, build Harbor Park, and win 6 leagues to reach the Pennant.
