# Pet Life sounds — credits

All sounds are real recordings, trimmed, cleaned and level-matched for Pet Life.

## ESC-50 dataset (Karol J. Piczak) — CC BY-NC 3.0
Clips originally from Freesound.org recordists. https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50
- Cat meows (one real cat per pet): Buster 5-172639, Man 1-56380, Galaxy 5-214759, Cloudy 3-95694/95/98
- Purr: the real 25 Hz purr from cat clip 5-169983 (meows filtered out, looped)
- Bella's barks: dog clip 2-114280 (one big dog); whine: 4-182395
- Bath bubbles: water_drops 5-261433, 5-259180, 3-164593; rinse/splash: pouring_water 1-79220, 5-212734
- Brushing: brushing_teeth 1-68628, 5-220939
- Yard sounds: chirping_birds 2-108760, 3-155577/78; crickets 4-171823, 2-125875, 3-129678
