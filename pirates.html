Pocket Pirates

Captain your own little ship across the Saltwater Isles. Sink rival pirates, raid treasure islands and storm the forts.

⛵ Steer with the wheel on the left. Sailing with the wind is fastest!
💥 Fire your port (left) or starboard (right) cannons. Cannons shoot out of the sides of your ship, so turn side-on to aim.
🏝 Sail into a glowing X to dig up treasure.
