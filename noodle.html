NOODLE RUSH

Run the busiest little ramen bar in town.
Boil noodles, pour broth, add toppings, then serve before the customers lose patience!

1️⃣ Tap a noodle box to drop noodles in a pot
2️⃣ When the pot glows green, tap it to move the noodles into the selected bowl. Don't let them overcook!
3️⃣ Tap a broth, then the toppings
4️⃣ Tap the customer to serve. Tap 🗑 to bin a bad bowl.
