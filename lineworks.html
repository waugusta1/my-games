How to play

Running a metro

Draw a line. Drag from one station to another, then keep dragging through more stations. Each colored token at the bottom is a line you can build.
Passengers know their shape. Each little shape waiting at a station wants to reach any station of that shape. They will change trains at stations where lines meet.
Extend and reroute. Drag the T-shaped end of a line to extend it, or drag back to shorten it. Drag the middle of a line onto a station to add it. Close a line on itself to make a loop.
Do not let stations overflow. A crowded station starts a timer. If it fills up, your metro closes. Every week you get a new train plus one upgrade: a line, a carriage, an interchange or tunnels for crossing water. Tap a line token to add trains and carriages.
