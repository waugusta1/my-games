How to drive

Brake slows down and reverses. In the air it tips the nose down.
Gas drives forward. In the air it tips the nose up. Too much on a hill flips you over.

Grab fuel cans before the tank runs dry and don't land on your head. Collect coins, pull flips for bonuses and spend it all in the garage.
