Floe

A penguin, a frozen lake, and no brakes. Endless levels.

Swipe (or use arrow keys) to slide. You glide until you hit a rock, stop on snow, or fall through open water. Get home to the igloo in as few moves as you can. Grab fish on the way for bonus hints.
